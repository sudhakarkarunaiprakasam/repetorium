## Demo video

`video/repetorium-demo-1min.mp4` is a 60-second, 1920 x 1080 H.264 recording of the live Repetorium Qt app, captured continuously from the app window. It follows the active pack through the dashboard, a lesson, three answered practice questions and their feedback, the skill map, Library, review scheduling, and a progress dashboard. It has a fixed frame with restrained chapter titles drawn from the landing-page copy, anchored consistently at the bottom left, a low-level ambient music bed, and no narration. The closing two seconds keep the closing tagline; there is no on-screen music credit. `video/poster.jpg` is a frame from the closing progress view.

The recording uses generated demo interactions and an isolated in-memory progress profile; it does not expose or alter the user's saved progress. The quiz answers are evaluated by the app against the active pack.
